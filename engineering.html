Measured numbers

  • HookFlow: 73 tests, ~93% line coverage, 5 CI jobs (schema, Postgres, tests, docker-build, dashboard).
  • HookFlow ingest: ~39 rps at p95 29ms for 1KB payloads (TestClient + SQLite — the transaction, not production Postgres).
  • Lexora: 35 tests; retrieval recall probe 3/3 at top-3.
  • neuDB: 32 tests; per-insert cost grows with file size (O(N) confirmed) — do not use past ~10K rows per table.
