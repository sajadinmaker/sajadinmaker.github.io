03 · vpnbox

A free VPN for Linux, no accounts and no keys. It watches the volunteer-run VPN Gate relays and ranks what's actually alive by ping and speed. You can narrow the list by country or city. It connects you through OpenVPN with a full tunnel and the DNS handled, all in one stdlib-only Python file.

  • Python 3.8+ (stdlib only)
  • OpenVPN
  • v1.0.0-beta4
